Dashboard Guide

Quick explanation for daily, weekly, monthly forecast, status meaning, and chart reading.

About This Dashboard

This dashboard provides parking demand forecasting for daily, weekly, and monthly periods. It helps management monitor parking occupancy, area conditions, and future parking demand.

Daily is for operational risk, weekly is for monitoring, and monthly is for planning trend analysis.

Daily Forecast

Daily forecast is used for operational risk monitoring.

Normal Area is still safe and available.
Warning Area is getting close to full capacity.
Full Area is full or has no remaining slot.

Weekly Forecast

Weekly forecast is used for monitoring demand contribution across several weeks.

Good Area has relatively low contribution/demand.
Moderate Area needs monitoring.
High Area has high contribution/demand and needs attention.

Monthly Forecast

Monthly forecast is used for planning and availability trend analysis. Monthly status is based on the Av. value shown in the table compared with the previous month.

Start First month used as comparison baseline.
Increase Av. value increased from previous month.
Decrease Av. value decreased from previous month.
Stable Av. value is relatively unchanged.

Area Performance

  • Daily: compares remaining available slots. Colors follow Normal, Warning, and Full.
  • Weekly: compares area contribution percentage. Colors follow Good, Moderate, and High.
  • Monthly: compares availability contribution percentage. Colors follow Start, Increase, Decrease, and Stable.

Forecast Preview

  • Daily: shows the next 7 days from the selected date.
  • Weekly: shows the next 7 weeks from the selected week.
  • Monthly: shows the next 7 months from the selected month.

Insights

  • Daily Insights: summarize Full and Warning records for operational action.
  • Weekly Insights: summarize High and Moderate records for monitoring.
  • Monthly Insights: summarize Increase and Decrease records for planning.

Simple Reading Concept

Daily is for operational risk, weekly is for monitoring, and monthly is for management planning. Therefore, the status labels are different in each mode.

Note: Daily uses Normal, Warning, and Full because it represents operational condition. Weekly uses Good, Moderate, and High because it monitors weekly demand contribution. Monthly uses Start, Increase, Decrease, and Stable because it monitors Av. trend compared with previous month.
